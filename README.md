ADVANCED DATA ANALYSIS AND VISUALIZATION
 
This project presents an end-to-end exploratory data analysis and dashboard visualization of De-Lite Electronics Sales record. The aim is to clean,analyse and uncover actionable insights into product performance, customer engagement, and sales reps performance to support management's decision making.

 # 📊 Dataset Overview

-  Total Records: 101
-  Fields: 8
-   Source: AINOW Learnlive LMS
-   Contains:
       -  Date, Region,
       -  Product, Category, Unit Sold
       -  Unit Price, Sales Rep and Customer

# 🔍 Objectives & Business Questions

This analysis answers the following critical questions:

   1.  What is the total revenue generated by the 4 regions?
   2.  How many products are listed under each category?
   3.  What is the total number of Sales Rep?
   4.  Which product generated the highest revenue?
   5.  Who are the Top 3 Customers by Unit Sold?
   6.  Which product is the highest Sold by Unit?
   7.  WHat is the revenue trend per Month and Quarter?
